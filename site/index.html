New, free and open source

Chinese pinyin input for macOS, Linux and Haiku

Whole sentences, shuangpin, and on-device AI on the Mac. What you type stays on your computer unless you turn on cloud candidates.

Install

Pick your system. Every download is signed; the Mac's is notarized by Apple.

macOS

macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

  1. Open the .pkg. It installs for you alone, with no admin password, and switches to it.
  2. Switch input methods with Control-Space or 🌐. A Shift tap switches 中 / 英.
  3. For the AI (Apple silicon): Settings… in the input menu → Download (about 1.4 GB, once).

It checks GitHub once a day for a new release and installs it while the Mac is idle; you can turn that off.

Debian and Ubuntu

Ubuntu 22.04+ or Debian 12+, amd64 or arm64.


				

				
    
				
    Arch and Fedora: a PKGBUILD and an rpm spec build from the release; see Install on Linux. Not in the AUR or Copr yet.

    What it does
    
			
    One engine with a native front end on each system, in the spirit of 搜狗拼音 and the macOS pinyin input.
    
			
    
				
    句
    Whole sentences
    Type wozaixuexizhongwen, get 我在学习中文: scored with word pairs and the word you typed before.
    
				
    ✦
    On-device AIMac
    On Apple silicon, a small model on your Mac puts the candidate that reads best first, and suggests what comes next in gray; Tab takes it.
    
				
    双
    Full pinyin and shuangpin
    拼音加加, 小鹤, 自然码, 微软 and 智能ABC. Abbreviations too: zgr → 中国人.
    
				
    v
    v-mode
    v123 → 壹佰贰拾叁, v1+2*3 → 7, v2026.11.7 → 2026年11月7日, today's date, and symbols by code.
    
				
    学
    Learns from you
    It remembers what you pick, offers what usually follows (联想), and forgets a word with Ctrl+Delete.
    
				
    繁
    The details
    简体 or 繁體, 模糊音, 中英混输, emoji, full-width punctuation, and Shift for 中 / 英.
