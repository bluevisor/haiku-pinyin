Haiku Pinyin is installed. Switch to it with Control-Space or 🌐, and tap Shift for 中 / 英. Settings, and the AI models' download, are under Settings… in the input menu in the menu bar.

The first time, macOS lets only you add it to your input sources: Haiku Pinyin opens System Settings and waits while you click Edit… next to Input Sources, then +, Chinese, Simplified › Haiku Pinyin, and Add. Then it switches to it.
